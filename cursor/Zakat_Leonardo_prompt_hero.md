# Leonardo — креатив РК «Закат на море» (ZK)

Модель: **Phoenix** / Photorealistic. Текст на картинку **не** ставить (оффер в объявлении).

Aspect: сначала **3:4**, потом тот же промпт **1:1** и **16:9**.

## Prompt (вставить в Leonardo)

Ultra-realistic lifestyle photograph, full-frame DSLR, 35mm lens, f/2.0, natural golden hour. A stylish couple in their early thirties standing alone on the polished white deck of a compact 10-meter white motor yacht at sea, private charter not a crowded tourist boat. The woman, about 32, long windblown dark-auburn hair, simple sleeveless sand-colored summer dress, looks toward the sunset with a calm unposed expression. The man, about 34, short dark hair, light stubble, white t-shirt and beige chinos, stands slightly behind her with one hand on the stainless steel railing. Black Sea evening, Sochi–Adler coast far on the horizon as a low dark shoreline, no city neon. Dramatic sunset: warm orange-gold sun just above the water, sky fading from peach to soft pink and deep blue, gentle ripples, a few other boats far away. Warm directional light on skin and hair, shallow depth of field, medium-long shot from slightly behind-side, cinematic but photographic, premium but local Black Sea rental mood. Empty deck except the couple, no crew in frame. No text, no logos, no watermarks.
